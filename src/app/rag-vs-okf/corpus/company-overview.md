# Company overview

SB Company builds software that helps mid-size retailers plan and track
inventory. We sell two products, Atlas and Beacon (see their product pages),
and we've been operating since 2021.

We earn money in two main ways: monthly subscription fees for Atlas and
Beacon, and usage-based fees when a customer processes more data than their
plan includes. The exact formula for combining these into a single revenue
number is covered in our revenue recognition documents, not here - this page
is just an introduction for new team members.
