# Office and support

SB Company's main office is at 400 Harbor Way, Suite 210, Oakland, CA. Most
of the team works from there a few days a week.

If you're a customer with an urgent issue, our support team's SLA is to
respond to critical tickets within 4 business hours. Everything else in
this document is just general onboarding info for new hires and isn't
related to product functionality.
