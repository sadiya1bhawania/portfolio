# Atlas

Atlas is SB Company's inventory planning product. It forecasts how much
stock a retailer should order for the next month based on past sales, and
flags items that are likely to run out or overstock.

Atlas is sold as a monthly subscription, priced per store location. Most
customers connect Atlas to their point-of-sale system so forecasts update
automatically each night.
