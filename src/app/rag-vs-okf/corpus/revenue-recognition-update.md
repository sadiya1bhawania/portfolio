# Revenue recognition policy update

As of November 2025, finance updated how total revenue is calculated. This
replaces the formula in the original revenue recognition policy.

Total Revenue = Subscription Revenue + Usage Revenue + Add-on Revenue - Refunds

- **Add-on Revenue** is new: it's the sum of one-time purchases for extra
  features, like advanced reporting.
- **Refunds** are now subtracted in the same month they are issued, instead
  of being handled as a separate line item later.

Subscription Revenue and Usage Revenue are calculated the same way as
before; see the original policy for those definitions. Note that
Subscription Revenue is sensitive to customer churn - see the churn
definition for how the two relate.
