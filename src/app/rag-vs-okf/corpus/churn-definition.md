# Churn definition

Churn rate for a given month is calculated as:

Churn Rate = Customers Lost During the Month / Customers at the Start of the Month

A customer counts as "lost" if they cancel both Atlas and Beacon and do not
resubscribe within 30 days.

Churn matters for revenue because Subscription Revenue is just the sum of
active plan fees that month. When a customer churns, their plan fee stops
being counted the very next month, so a higher churn rate directly lowers
next month's Subscription Revenue - and therefore lowers total revenue, as
described in the revenue recognition update.
