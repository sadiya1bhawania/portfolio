# Beacon

Beacon is SB Company's inventory tracking product. It gives retailers a
live view of stock levels across all their store locations and warehouses,
and alerts staff when an item's count looks wrong.

Beacon is also sold as a monthly subscription. Customers who process a
large volume of stock updates each day may go over their plan's included
usage and get charged an overage fee, which counts as usage revenue.
