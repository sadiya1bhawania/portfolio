# Revenue recognition policy

This is the original policy, written when SB Company only tracked two
revenue components.

Total revenue for a given month is calculated as:

Total Revenue = Subscription Revenue + Usage Revenue

- **Subscription Revenue** is the sum of active monthly plan fees for every
  customer on Atlas or Beacon that month.
- **Usage Revenue** is the sum of overage charges billed when a customer's
  data processing goes over their plan's included amount.

See the revenue recognition update for a newer version of this formula.
