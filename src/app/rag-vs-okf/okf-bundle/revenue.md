### Revenue Calculation
Total monthly revenue is calculated as follows:

Total Revenue = Subscription Revenue + Usage Revenue + Add-on Revenue - Refunds

- **Subscription Revenue**: The sum of active monthly plan fees for all customers using Atlas or Beacon. This is directly impacted by customer churn.
- **Usage Revenue**: The sum of overage charges billed when a customer exceeds their plan's data processing limits.
- **Add-on Revenue**: The sum of one-time purchases for extra features, such as advanced reporting.
- **Refunds**: Deducted from the total in the same month they are issued.
