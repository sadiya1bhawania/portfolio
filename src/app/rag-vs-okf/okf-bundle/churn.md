### Churn Definition
Churn rate is calculated as: (Customers Lost During the Month) / (Customers at the Start of the Month).

A customer is considered "lost" if they cancel both Atlas and Beacon subscriptions and do not resubscribe within 30 days. Because Subscription Revenue is the sum of active plan fees, a higher churn rate directly reduces Subscription Revenue in the following month.
